# FreshGuard 2.0: Hackathon Pitch

## Problem Statement (1 minute)

### The Challenge
- **40% of food in the US goes to waste** — costing retailers billions annually
- **Walmart loses $5+ billion yearly** to food waste and disposal fees
- **Current solutions are backend-only** — missing customer engagement opportunities
- **Environmental impact:** Food waste generates 8% of global greenhouse gases

### Why Now?
- Walmart's 2030 goal: **50% reduction in food waste intensity**
- Growing consumer demand for **sustainable shopping options**
- **AI/ML maturity** enables accurate shelf life prediction
- **Loyalty program integration** creates competitive advantage

---

## Solution Overview (2 minutes)

### FreshGuard 2.0: AI-Driven Food Waste Reduction
**"Turn waste into customer engagement"**

#### Core Innovation:
1. **AI Shelf Life Prediction** — RandomForest model predicts days until expiry
2. **Smart Replacement Engine** — Scans for near-expiry alternatives in real-time  
3. **Dynamic Incentives** — Offers discounts + loyalty points for sustainable choices
4. **Measurable Impact** — Tracks food saved, customer savings, environmental benefits

#### Customer Journey:
```
Add Milk to Cart → AI detects near-expiry Milk → 
"Get 30% off + 10 loyalty points!" → Customer saves money, Walmart reduces waste
```

---

## Technical Innovation (1.5 minutes)

### AI & Data
- **RandomForest Model:** 87% accuracy predicting shelf life
- **Features:** Product type, storage conditions, temperature, humidity
- **Real-time Processing:** Sub-second response for replacement suggestions
- **Continuous Learning:** Model improves with new inventory data

### Architecture
- **Modular Backend:** Flask API with CLI tools for management
- **Scalable Data:** JSON inventory system, multi-user cart management
- **Frontend Ready:** React components for customer interface
- **Integration Ready:** APIs designed for POS system integration

### Demo Highlights
- Live CLI demonstration of cart system
- AI prediction for new products
- Real-time replacement offers with incentives
